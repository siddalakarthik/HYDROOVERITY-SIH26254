# Project charter

## Identity and purpose

HYDROOVERITY, Team TARK (155480), Smart India Hackathon 2026, SIH26254, Hardware / Clean & Green Technology, Ministry of Jal Shakti.

Problem statement: Development of Indigenous Leak Detection Sensor Systems for Operation & Maintenance of Water Supply Pipelines.

Mission: **Find the leak. Understand the evidence. Verify the repair.** Help rural/small-town utilities detect and investigate water-distribution leakage, prioritize maintenance and demonstrate accountable repair under intermittent supply and limited resources.

## Stakeholders and deliverables

The engineering owner approves milestone scope, architecture changes, licensing and expenditure. Administrators manage access; utility engineers inspect topology/evidence; supervisors triage/assign/review; technicians investigate/service/repair; residents report and track their own service concerns.

Deliver a private engineering repository, functional multi-role website/backend, reproducible scientific simulators, digital mechanical/electrical/embedded designs, tests and traceable evidence, and a labeled 2–3 minute concept/demo video plus final PPT. Current work is M01 infrastructure/documentation only. Physical purchase/build is deferred.

## Budget

Material ceiling: ₹60,000. Estimated complete potential demonstration allocation: rig ₹19,900; GUARD-5 ₹4,600; WATCH/slow electronics ₹8,800; EAR ₹12,550; common fabrication/interface ₹5,900; contingency ₹5,000. Total ₹56,750; headroom ₹3,250. Supplier quotations and the detailed 26-line BOM are unavailable here. Reconcile overlap, ratings, freight/GST and replacement allowance before procurement. No spending authorization is implied.

This cap excludes labor, laptop, software services and filmmaking. It is not a government allowance, entitlement, per-sensor price or village deployment estimate. Model field installation, connectivity, batteries and maintenance separately.

## Scope and success boundaries

Retain WATCH, five-channel GUARD-5, shared-clock EAR-PAIR, evidence decisions and VERIFY. No universal buried-network pinpointing, contamination sensing or autonomous municipal actuation. No measured accuracy, battery life, savings or patent novelty established. Compare against competent baselines; retain uncertainty and failures.

Completion means working authorized workflows, persistence, access control, error states, appropriate executed tests and honest documentation. The Hardware-category submission remains at risk while physical validation is absent; animation is not a hardware trial.
